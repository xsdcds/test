# PaperGate-IEEE FAQ

## Is PaperGate affiliated with IEEE?
No. PaperGate-IEEE is an independent tool and service. Always follow the target venue's current official author instructions.

## What does the free tool do?
It performs deterministic heuristic checks over a LaTeX manuscript for submission hygiene, contribution positioning, experiment evidence, industrial relevance, and reliability/calibration cues.

## Does the free score predict acceptance?
No. It is a preflight risk score, not an acceptance probability.

## What is the difference between the free scanner and Pro audit?
The free scanner catches rule-based signals. The Pro audit reasons across the manuscript: whether the claimed novelty is actually visible, whether the method and experiments form a closed argument, what a skeptical reviewer is likely to attack, and which revisions have the highest expected impact.

## Quick Risk Audit vs Full Reviewer Audit
**Quick Risk Audit — ¥49 / US$7**
- top 5 reviewer attack points
- novelty/contribution clarity
- most important missing evidence
- revise/submit recommendation

**Full Reviewer Audit — ¥99 / US$15**
- full prioritized issue ledger
- method-logic review
- experiment-closure review
- reliability/calibration review when relevant
- industrial relevance/deployment review
- figure/table effectiveness
- likely reviewer objections

## Do I need to upload my manuscript publicly?
No. Do not post unpublished or confidential manuscripts in public GitHub issues. Public issues are only for non-sensitive scoping information. Private exchange can be arranged after scope is agreed.

## Will you rewrite experiments or invent results?
No. The service does not fabricate experiments, citations, data, or claims.

## Can the audit guarantee acceptance?
No. It identifies avoidable weaknesses and reviewer risks; editorial and peer-review decisions remain outside the service's control.

## What manuscripts are a good fit?
IEEE-style journal/conference work in industrial AI, edge intelligence, reliability, condition monitoring, PHM, robotics, and related intelligent systems is the strongest fit.

## How do I request an audit?
Open a **Pro manuscript audit request** issue and provide the target venue, title/anonymized title, abstract or short summary, and the main concern. Do not include confidential material.
